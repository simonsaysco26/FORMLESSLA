# Formless LA — Design System

## Brand
Creative studio in Los Angeles. Event production and 
experiential marketing. Clients include Porsche, Red Bull, 
Soho House, Charli XCX, Billie Eilish. Long-term vision: 
brick-and-mortar coworking space for LA creatives.

## Vibe
Editorial, photo-forward, confident. Creative studio meets 
gallery. Not corporate, not cluttered.

## Colors
- Primary background: near-black with grainy/noise texture (#0F0F0F base)
- Secondary background: light warm concrete texture (#D9D4CC base)
- Primary text on dark: #FFFFFF
- Primary text on light: #0F0F0F
- Accent: use sparingly, keep monochrome-forward

## Typography
- Headline font: bold condensed sans-serif (like "OUR STORY" 
  and "VISION" on the current site)
- Body font: clean sans-serif, regular weight
- Use BIG confident sizes for headlines
- All caps for major headings

## Textures
- Do NOT use flat colors for backgrounds
- Dark sections: subtle grain/noise texture
- Light sections: warm concrete/paper texture
- Textures carry the brand — never flatten them

## Layout
- Generous whitespace
- Alternate dark and light textured sections for rhythm
- Photo-first — let images do the heavy lifting
- Desktop-first but fully mobile-responsive

## Components
- Nav: Home, About, Clients, Photos, Contact + Instagram icon
- Buttons: minimal, bold condensed type
- Links: underline on hover
- Animations: smooth fade-ins only, no flashy effects
- Client logo marquee: continuous horizontal scroll, infinite loop

## Voice
Human, real, confident. Not agency-speak. Hints at the future 
brick-and-mortar vision ("building toward a physical home for 
LA creatives").
